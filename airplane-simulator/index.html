AirportSim

A terminal airport simulator written in C++17 from scratch, with hand-written queues instead of the STL ones. Below are real runs of the program played back: arrivals hold until a runway frees up, planes low on fuel jump the line, and departures queue on the taxiway and take off while the sun sets. Hover a plane to see its flight.

    ⚡
    
                
    Priority queue
    
                
    Sorted linked list for arrivals. Emergency planes (<20% fuel) jump to the front. Insertion O(n), dequeue O(1).
    
            
    
            
    
                
    →
    
                
    FIFO queue
    
                
    Standard linked list for departures. First in, first out. Enqueue and dequeue both O(1). No STL container used.
    
            
    
            
    
                
    🛬
    
                
    Fuel emergencies
    
                
    Arriving planes burn 1% fuel per minute while waiting. Below 20% triggers EMERGENCY priority. At 0% the plane crashes.
    
            
    
            
    
                
    🛫
    
                
    Runway state machine
    
                
    Each runway cycles: AVAILABLE → LANDING/TAKEOFF → AVAILABLE. Arrivals always take priority over departures.
